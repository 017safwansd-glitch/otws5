## Bangladesh heat statistic

The World Bank report *An Unsustainable Life: The Impact of Heat on Health and the Economy of Bangladesh* states that Bangladesh's feels-like temperature increased by 4.5°C between 1980 and 2023.

- Report: https://documents.worldbank.org/pt/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099111024033034518
- World Bank release, 16 September 2025: https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/09/16/bangladesh-faces-health-and-economic-risks-from-rising-temperature-world-bank
- World Bank explanation: https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/endpovertyinsouthasia/when-heat-drains-growth--the-human-cost-of-rising-temperatures-i
