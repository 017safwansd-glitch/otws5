# Visual strategy and choreography

The previous cut asked the viewer to reset attention 43 times. This cut groups related evidence into ten sustained scenes. New information joins an existing spatial arrangement. The viewer can compare what they just heard with what comes next.

| Time | Scene | Deliberate motion | Connection |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–23 | Outdoor discomfort | A collar droplet appears; three discomfort cues accumulate. | Establish the droplet and the person, rather than separate title cards for each symptom. |
| 23–51 | Humidity and evidence | Vapour rises once; humidity accumulates and surface droplets remain. The heat statistic then joins the same image. | The collar droplet moves toward the same skin surface used later for the formula. |
| 51–80 | Existing routines | Three categories enter as narration reaches them, then stay. No fan spin or spray particle loops. | One comparative landscape allows the viewer to see all the limitations together. The droplet remains as the unresolved problem. |
| 80–94 | Market gap | The recurring droplet moves onto the line linking sweat and relief. | Sweat is reframed from discomfort to a possible trigger. |
| 94–106 | ReCool reveal | One restrained bottle entrance; use instructions join the product. | The droplet moves toward the pack, linking the question to the proposed solution. |
| 106–122 | Proposed mechanism | Reservoirs appear, the droplet approaches, retained agents move out once, and the cooling field appears. | The same skin baseline closes the explanatory loop begun in humidity. Diagram is conceptual, subject to testing. |
| 122–143 | Brand and packs | Both packs appear together; the emotional promise and proposed prices join at narrated cues. | The product silhouette continues from the reveal and establishes the closing arrangement. |
| 143–157 | Launch | Bangladesh map stays; cities and three launch methods build beside it. | National scale becomes a concrete two-city launch. |
| 157–170 | Business case | Three final-value figures enter once and remain. | Retail opportunity leads to sales and EBITDA, all visible for comparison. No misleading fabricated growth chart. |
| 170–180 | Closing | Opening heat colour returns; packs settle once; tagline enters and holds. | Close the heat/preparation story with the same product pair. |

Nine 0.7-second dissolves. No wipes, liquid irises, per-word kinetic effects, numeric count-ups, ambient loops, persistent chrome or interview placeholder. Motion accompanies an explanation rather than decorating every second. Holds are intentional.
